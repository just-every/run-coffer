Every gift is an exact line in a ledger, in whole cents. Gifts are never edited after they are recorded; a mistake is corrected with a reversal, so the history always adds up.

## Record a gift

On the donor's page, fill in **Record a gift**:

- **Amount** in dollars, such as `250.00`.
- **Received on**, the date the gift arrived. It cannot be in the future.
- **Paid by**: check, card, online, cash, bank transfer or other.
- **Check number or reference** (optional). A reference can be used only once among hand-entered gifts, which stops the same check being entered twice.
- **Fund**, Unrestricted unless you name another.
- **Toward pledge**, when the donor has an open pledge.
- **Did the donor receive goods or services?** Choose **No goods or services**, or **Yes** with their value and what they received, such as a dinner ticket. The acknowledgement letter states this answer.
- A **Note**, if you like.

**Record gift** adds the line. Gifts at or over your threshold show **Letter due** until a letter goes out.

## Pledges

Open **Record a pledge** on the donor's page with the amount, the date pledged, an optional due-by date and the fund. Gifts recorded **Toward pledge** count against it, and the page shows what has been paid and what is still to come.

## Correct a gift

An owner or admin opens **Reverse this gift** under the gift, gives a reason (for example "Refunded to donor") and presses **Reverse gift**. Coffer adds a matching negative line with the reason. Totals, the year figures and letters follow the ledger. A gift can be reversed once, and not while it sits in a letter batch that is prepared or approved.

## Goods or services not answered

Gifts moved in from Stripe without the **These charges were donations** box show **Goods/services not answered**. An owner or admin opens **Answer goods or services** under the gift and saves the answer; until then that gift's letter waits.
