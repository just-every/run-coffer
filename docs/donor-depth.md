# Relationships, conversations and commitments

On **Donors**, filter by relationship type or by the donor at the other end of a connection. Each donor record links both directions: spouse, employee/employer, board member/organization, foundation officer/foundation, and introduced by/introduced. Calls, meetings and emails record the date, participants, notes, next step and staff author beside gifts in the timeline.

Gift recognition is separate from money received. A soft credit appears on the recognized donor and the original gift, but never changes received totals or generates a tax receipt for the recognized donor. Reversing the original gift removes its active recognition; processor refunds reduce recognition to the contribution still held. A tribute records honor or memory, the honoree and an optional notification contact. Record it before preparing receipts; approved per-gift and year-end receipt text includes the tribute. Notification contacts are stored for staff; notification emails are not yet sent.

An employer match records an expected amount and deadline against the original gift. Record the employer's actual payment as its own gift, then link it from the employer record. Expected and overdue matches add no received income; received matches refer to the actual ledger gift. Reversing either payment updates the state.

First gift is the earliest unreversed ledger gift, with amount, date and appeal. Staff gifts accept an appeal name; Stripe move-in carries `metadata.appeal` or `metadata.campaign`, and online gifts use their campaign name. Open **Review possible duplicates** on Donors, or **Possible duplicates** under More. The queue explains shared emails, phone numbers, a matching name and postal address, and similar names across records entered by staff, imported or created by online giving. People in the same household stay separate, including spouses sharing an email. An unrelated person cannot be added with a current email already in use.

Compare records side by side, choose the surviving record and pick each contact field to keep. An owner or admin combines the histories without changing gift amounts, dates or references. Source IDs and both original emails stay as import aliases. Conversations, notes, relationships, consent, pledges, recognition and provider links follow the survivor; later imports and gifts find that same record.

The merge opens its **Audit log** entry. **Undo merge** restores the original contact values and the precise record ownership while all affected records remain unchanged; it refuses rather than overwriting a later gift, edit, send or import. Undo nested merges newest first. Private donor links stay revoked, cancelled welcome steps stay stopped and provider preferences need a fresh check. Erasure removes private merge snapshots and makes undo unavailable. Unsent prepared or approved letters and appeals must be discarded before merging, and a message already being submitted must finish first.

Pledges have a total and dated installments, entered one per line as `YYYY-MM-DD | amount`. Dates must be distinct, amounts must total the pledge, and each installment has its own balance. Select an installment when recording a gift, or match a previously recorded/imported gift. Allocations cannot exceed either balance and must use the same donor and fund. Reversals and processor refunds reopen the balance without editing gifts.

On self-hosted installs with SendGrid configured, the Worker checks due reminders daily at 13:00 UTC, using the organization's time zone to determine the calendar day. Staff can also send due reminders from a pledge. Current email permission, address and known bounces are checked before submission. Each installment has one durable submission attempt; uncertain or rejected submissions remain visible for staff and are never retried automatically. Provider acceptance is shown as submitted, not delivered. Paid installments are skipped. RER Managed email sending is not available yet; development and demo suppress mail.

The authenticated `/api/coffer/commands` endpoint uses the same commands as the forms: `relationship.create`, `relationship.remove`, `interaction.record`, `softCredit.record`, `softCredit.remove`, `tribute.record`, `match.record`, `match.receive`, `pledge.create`, `pledge.remind`, `pledge.match` and manager-only `donor.merge` and `donor.unmerge`. The authenticated `/api/coffer/duplicates` endpoint supports `donorId` and `page`; `/api/coffer/duplicates/compare?source=ID&target=ID` supplies versions and contact choices for a reviewed merge. `/api/coffer/donors/:id` includes `depth`, `giftDepth`, `firstGift` and installment balances. Agent keys cannot run these writes. Backup/restore includes the new records. Erasure removes relationship/conversation and tribute contact data, redacts affected frozen receipt text and clears reminder recipient/provider identifiers.

The two new public-door tests are `tests/pledges.test.mjs` (reminder submission, matching, balance, reversal and uncertain-submission handling) and `tests/soft-credits.test.mjs` (ledger separation, tribute receipts, match receipts/reversals and erasure). They fake the paid mail edge only.
