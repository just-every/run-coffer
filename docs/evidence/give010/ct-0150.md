**Verdict: APPROVE WITH RIDERS.** An uncertain submission may hold later recovery contact across that donor’s invoices and plans. It must have a usable, attributed exit: investigated closure deliberately stops contact for the affected plan, preserves uncertainty, and releases other independently eligible plans from that particular hold. Closing one uncertainty must not clear another unresolved submission.

A deliberate final close may remain final for that plan. The product must distinguish that decision from a donor left indefinitely “awaiting investigation.”

This preserves the category’s real recovery job. Givebutter combines failed-payment notices, retries and a payment-update route; Bloomerang likewise supports updating recurring payment information without automatically collecting missed payments. A communication hold can coexist with processor-owned retries and a workable recovery route. [Givebutter recurring plans](https://help.givebutter.com/en/articles/3216251-how-to-enable-and-manage-recurring-plans), [Bloomerang payment updates](https://help.bloomerang.com/en/articles/13382777-how-do-i-update-a-recurring-donor-s-payment-information).
