**Donors** lists everyone with their total given, last gift and consent for email and postal mail, 50 to a page. Search by name, email or household.

## Add a donor

Fill in **Add a donor** on the Donors page: a name, and optionally email, phone, household, address and country (United States by default). An email address belongs to one donor only. **Add donor** opens their page.

## Households

Type a household name, such as "Okafor household", on each member's details. Coffer finds the household by name (in any case) or creates it, and each member's page lists the others with a link.

## A donor's page

A donor's page shows, in one place:

- what they have given in total and in each of the last three years;
- every gift as a ledger line, with its fund, how it was paid, any note, and whether its letter is due, prepared or sent;
- pledges, with what has been paid and what is still to come;
- their contact details and household;
- the full history of their consent;
- the letters and appeals they were part of;
- an activity list of every change, who made it and when.

## Edit contact details

Open **Edit contact details**, change what you need and **Save details**. Every edit is versioned: if someone else saved the donor first, Coffer asks you to refresh rather than overwrite their change.
