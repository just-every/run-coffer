# Calls, major gifts and retention

From Today, open Lapses to see LYBUNT (last calendar year but no positive net giving this year), earlier-year SYBUNT, and stopped monthly plans. Each row explains the flag and its annual reference amount at risk. Current-year lapses are provisional. Monthly plans use their own linked paid gifts: more than 45 days since the last positive-balance gift, or a past-due, unpaid or canceled provider status. Legacy plans without linked gifts use provider status alone and say the last paid date is unknown. Annual lapse rows are excluded for donors already represented by stopped monthly plans, so risk totals do not double count them. Risk is a reference amount, not a debt or forecast.

Create a follow-up task on a lapse, or choose an unpaid due pledge installment in Calls. Assign it to an active staff member with a due date. Repeated creation preserves the existing task. Calls defaults to your open and snoozed tasks; filter by owner or completed status. Change assignment/due date, snooze until a future date, or log the call outcome and next step. Completion and the conversation entry on the donor timeline save together. The task's risk amount is a creation-time snapshot; review the live donor before calling. Tasks for erased donors are removed, and donor merges preserve tasks and opportunities.

Pipeline records each prospect's owner, stage (identify, qualify, cultivate, ask, stewardship), ask amount, expected close and next step. Filter by stage or owner. Expand engagement to see inputs and points for recency, giving frequency, giving amount and recorded conversations. The displayed formula is deterministic and uses the last 365 calendar days; refunded gifts do not score. Multiple opportunities for one donor stay separate. Planned asks never become received gifts.

Retention compares a complete prior calendar year with the selected year (year to date for the current year). Retained, new, lapsed and recaptured groups are mutually exclusive and use positive net received contributions. Every count and amount links to donor rows. Refund-only and fully offset donors appear separately to reconcile signed amounts without inflating retention counts. Erased/merged alias identities are excluded from identifiable cohorts; their amounts reconcile separately to the contribution ledger. Fees and soft credits are excluded from donor contributions.

Private read APIs: `/api/coffer/lapses`, `/api/coffer/tasks`, `/api/coffer/prospects`, `/api/coffer/retention?year=2026`. Writes use `/api/coffer/commands`: `task.generate`, `task.assign`, `task.snooze`, `task.complete`, `prospect.save`. Staff writes recheck current authority in the database transaction. Updates carry a version to refuse stale forms. These features need no external provider calls; the existing Stripe webhook supplies monthly-plan state and paid-gift associations on self-hosted installs. Provider integrations on RER Managed remain not available yet.

The broader contract still includes work outside these claims: automatic weekly ranking, team capacity and outcome attribution, recurring tasks, reminders/escalations, full portfolio analytics, configurable score weights, fiscal-year/fund/campaign retention and closed-won gift linkage. The contract marks those clauses partial.

Checks: `tests/pipeline.test.mjs` exercises an owned lapse call through reassignment, snooze and donor-timeline completion, an overdue pledge, pipeline movement and score inputs, plus donor-level retention reconciliation. `tests/giving.test.mjs` exercises stopped monthly status, linked paid history and recovery through signed Stripe webhooks with only network edges faked.

## Repeating tasks (STEW-002)

In **Calls → Repeating tasks**, create a monthly or quarterly team task with a title, owner and first due date. For a board-member check-in, open the donor first and choose **Create a repeating task**; the task stays linked to that donor. General retention reviews need no donor record.

Complete an occurrence with notes to create its successor in the same transaction. If its due date passes first, the minute scheduler creates the next occurrence and keeps the overdue one open for follow-up. Completing that overdue task later never duplicates its successor. Each interval is measured from the prior due date, keeping the original day after shorter months (January 31 → February 28 → March 31). Open and completed occurrences follow the Calls owner/status filters. Backups include the series and completion history; donor merges move linked tasks, undo restores them, and donor erasure removes them.

Staff HTTP doors are `task.repeatCreate`, `task.repeatComplete` and `GET /api/coffer/recurring-tasks` (optional `owner` query). These commands do not grant agent-key access.
