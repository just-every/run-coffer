# Acceptance

A development lead at a US nonprofit replacing Bloomerang, Raiser's Edge NXT, Little Green Light, Neon CRM, Givebutter or Zeffy can do everything below. Each item names the clause in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) it proves and that clause's status in CONTRACT.md's sole-authority Status table. A build round works through the `partial` and `missing` items; an item is accepted only when its evidence runs through a public door.

## First release guarantees

1. **`IMPORT-001` Reviewed Stripe move-in** (done). An HTTP move-in journey previews failed and non-USD skips, a partially refunded charge with gross 10,000, refunded 2,500 and net 7,500 cents, and a fully refunded charge with gross and refunded 10,000 cents and net zero retained as refunded. It rejects an upload with repeated IDs within the file and one with already-imported IDs, names every ID and row, and verifies that neither preview imports anything; the owner fixes the file before apply. It refuses a wrong fingerprint, applies the exact reviewed plan, reads the original pages and refund facts in the journal and undoes; a second journey proves undo stops at a donor a person has since changed.
2. See each donor's history: gifts by year as exact ledger lines (corrections are reversals), pledges, consent, letters and appeals. (`DONOR-001`, done; `GIFT-001`, done). The ledger test records exact-cent gifts, refuses sub-cent, future-dated and duplicate-reference gifts, reverses one once and reads the lifetime and per-year totals back. An HTTP journey refuses direct donor reassignment, merges two donors, undoes the merge and erases a donor; after each permitted reassignment it compares amount, date, fund and receipt with the originals and reads the actor and reason in the audit record. Undo restores gift ownership; erasure removes personal data and makes merge undo unavailable.
3. Acknowledge gifts of $250 or more on time: an agent or person prepares letters in the charity's own wording, a person approves, exports them and records when they went out. (`ACK-001`, partial; `AGENT-001`, done). The acknowledgment test has an agent prepare a batch, refuses preparation before settings are reviewed, lets only a person approve, exports the CSV with the exact letter text and records the sent date. It checks imported goods values, excludes gifts imported as acknowledged or do not acknowledge and gifts before the acknowledge-from date, and reads each sent gift as acknowledged by letter with its batch linked.
4. Record an appeal only to donors whose current consent for that channel is yes; a change in consent stops approval and export. (`CONSENT-001`, `APPEAL-001`, done)
5. Keep custody: back up and restore into an empty installation, and erase a donor's personal data without losing the gift ledger or reviving them from an old backup or a later import. (`DATA-001`, done)
6. Every approval records who prepared and who approved the exact content. Roles are enforced on every server request. (`HTTP-001`, `AGENT-001`, done)
