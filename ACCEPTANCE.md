# Acceptance

A development lead at a US nonprofit replacing Bloomerang, Raiser's Edge NXT, Little Green Light, Neon CRM, Givebutter or Zeffy can do everything below. Each item names the clause in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) it proves and that clause's status in CONTRACT.md's sole-authority Status table. A build round works through the `partial` and `missing` items; an item is accepted only when its evidence runs through a public door.

(`ACK-001`, done; `AGENT-001`, done). 5. Keep custody: back up and restore into an empty installation, and erase a donor's personal data without losing the gift ledger or reviving them from an old backup or a later import. (`DATA-001`, done)
6. Every approval records who prepared and who approved the exact content. Roles are enforced on every server request. (`HTTP-001`, `AGENT-001`, done)
